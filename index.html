How to Play Scripture Phrase

1

Sit in a Circle: Divide your youth group or class into 2 teams. Sit in a circle alternating teams (Team 1, Team 2, Team 1, Team 2).

2

Give Clues: The player holding the device describes the Bible or Lutheran phrase on screen using any helpful clues, actions, or context (just don't say the exact words printed on the card!).

3

Pass It Fast: As soon as your teammates shout the right answer, tap CORRECT and immediately hand the device to the player on your left (the other team).

4

Beat the Buzzer: The timer ticks faster and faster! Whichever team is stuck holding the device when the buzzer goes off loses the round, and the other team scores 1 point.
